## How to schedule-send in Gmail (Sun Aug 2 / Mon Aug 3)

1. Compose the email; fill in recipient, subject, body.
2. Click the small **▾ arrow next to the blue Send button**.
3. Choose **Schedule send**.
4. Pick **Mon, Aug 3, 8:00 AM** (or Sun, Aug 2). "Pick date & time" for an exact
   slot.
5. Repeat for each. They wait in the **Scheduled** folder — you can edit or
   cancel any of them right up until they send.
